Double blinkHow often a blink comes as a quick pair

Faces that animate themselves keep their own blinking, so this only appears for faces the mask can take over — this one, and anything you draw as a single frame. Mask blinks blinks at random gaps of 2.5 to 7 seconds instead of on a fixed cycle, and takes about the same 450 ms the default face's own blink does. Double blink sets how many of those come as a quick pair — a second blink a tenth of a second behind the first, which is what stops it reading as a machine ticking. 0 turns pairs off.

How to set it up

1. With nothing near the visor, press Calibrate. Closeness should sit near 0.

2. Hold your hand on the snout and watch Closeness. Set Boop distance about halfway between resting and your hand.

3. In normal light, your hand should push Shadow well past its yellow line. If it doesn't, lower Shadow needed.

In the dark there's no light to shade, so boops go on closeness alone and the Shadow bar greys out. Random boops that are ignored are counted at the top right of the meter.

How it works

The panels shine into the light sensor, so a reading with them on partly measures the panels themselves. The methods get round that differently:

Blank switches the panels off for about a quarter of a second at each check and reads the room without them. The most accurate, at the cost of a short blink.

Hybrid watches the reading with the panels on, which costs nothing, and only blanks when the light seems to have changed by a quarter or more (and every tenth check regardless). Few blinks, and it still reacts when you walk outside.

Live never blinks. It uses a smoothed reading with the panels on, so the panels' own glow pushes it a little brighter. Fine if the sensor is shielded from the panels.

To calibrate: in the darkest place you'll wear it, press Measure, then Use now on Dark point. Do the same in bright light for Bright point. Brightness is spread between Lowest and Highest on the way between the two. It never blinks over a face change or a boop.

If the mouth doesn't move

Watch Mic level while you talk. No signal means the reading never moves at all, so the mask isn't hearing the mic: check its OUT wire on GPIO 4, and that it's powered from 3V3 rather than the panel 5V.

Moving but never reaching the line: turn the small screw pot on the mic board up, or lower Trigger point. Always over the line: turn the pot down, then press Measure silence again.

Only faces with mouth panels move, and speech is ignored while a boop or the sleeping face is showing.
