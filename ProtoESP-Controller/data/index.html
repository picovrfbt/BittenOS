Advanced settings

Boop: the sensor always sees some of its own light reflecting off the inside of the visor, so first press Calibrate with nothing near the visor — that measures the resting level and takes it off the readings. Then press Read with nothing near (it should be close to 0) and again with your hand on the visor, and set Boop distance between the two. A boop triggers when the reading rises above it.

If Read still shows a large number with nothing near the visor after calibrating, the visor is reflecting too much straight back into the sensor. Check Boop reading on the System card: if raw is near 255 there is no room left to detect anything, and the sensor needs a light barrier between its LED and its detector, or to sit closer to the visor with no air gap.

Change the WiFi name if other ProtoESP protogens are nearby, so their remotes don't connect to yours. You'll need to reconnect after the mask restarts.
